01 Identity — mark · color · type

The mark

chowk.

A map tile: four city blocks around a crossroads. The marigold dot is the chowk itself — the point where everything meets. The dot recurs as the wordmark's full stop and as small active-state accents across the product.

Color — paper, peepal, one marigold dot

Paper#FAF7F0ground
Ink#211E1Atext · primary CTA
Peepal#1B7F4Dbrand · pins · interactive
Peepal deep#136640links · pressed · badges
Marigold#F5B301the dot · selected
Stone#B8AE9Equiet pins · disabled

Peepal green is the tree at the center of every Indian square — it marks the brand, the pins, and everything you can act on. Marigold is the garland: one dot of emphasis per view. One green, one yellow, ink for buttons — the whole system in a sentence.
